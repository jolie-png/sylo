## Describe the problem you identified, your solution, and the impact it would have

**The problem**

Every student knows internships matter. They know research experience matters, that fellowships open doors. Awareness isn't the problem. **Connecting the dots is.**

The path exists, but for most students it stays invisible. So much comes down to hearing the right thing from the right person at the right time — a professor who mentions a fellowship, an upperclassman who forwards a deadline, a flyer you pass. Miss that moment and you're in the dark: you never knew the opportunity existed, never knew you qualified, never knew there was a next step. They can't see the sequence either — the research program this fall that makes them competitive for a fellowship next spring, which unlocks the internship that follows.

Even when the information is findable, it's scattered — a deadline on a university page, an internship in a LinkedIn post, a flyer for a research opening. Students screenshot these things and lose them in a camera roll of thousands, then miss the window entirely. Not for lack of qualification — for lack of a map.

I remember recruiting my sophomore year and feeling buried by all of it — so many things to weigh against the one goal I cared about: landing an internship in the industry I wanted. What I needed was a through-line to that goal — one place that pulled together the opportunities scattered everywhere and turned them into a plan I could actually follow.

**Our solution**

Sylo draws that map — personalized to *you*. A student shares their goal, major, year, and school, and optionally a resume. Sylo builds a roadmap for their exact situation: a Biology sophomore at UCLA sees different programs than a CS junior at Georgia Tech. Every step is a real opportunity *at their own school*, sequenced by what it builds on, what it opens, and when it closes — plus a gap analysis naming what they have, what's missing, and the highest-leverage next move.

**Pin Drop** keeps the map alive. A student drops in any screenshot from the wild. Claude's vision model reads it in seconds, extracts the deadline and requirements, checks eligibility, and files it — one tap adds it to their roadmap. Instead of scattered screenshots and saved links, everything gets read, sorted, and organized automatically.

Everything flows into a **progress board** where AI steps and saved pins sit side-by-side, dragged between Not Started, In Progress, and Complete. **Success Maps** show real roadmaps from students who made it, and the key opportunities in those paths flow into a **Curated Opportunities Database** — so you can apply to the same programs that got them there.

**The impact**

Sylo turns what used to depend on luck and access into something anyone can open in a browser. Handshake, career centers, and university portals each cover part of this — job listings, advising hours, program pages. Sylo adds the part that connects them: it reasons over a student's major, year, and school to sequence those pieces into a single next move.

When the map is shared, opportunity isn't a matter of who heard first — it's a matter of who's ready to move. That's the map we're building for everyone.

Built on TanStack Start and Claude with no sign-up, Sylo takes a student from "I know I should be doing something" to a personalized, actionable plan in under a minute.
