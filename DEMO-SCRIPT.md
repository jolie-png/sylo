# Sylo — 2-Minute Demo Script

**Target length:** ~2:00 (aim for 300–330 spoken words)
**Host on:** Loom, Vimeo, or Vidyard
**Recording setup:** Screen + mic. Have the app open and warmed up. Use the **instant demo persona** (Maya · CS @ Georgia Tech) so you skip typing and jump straight to a populated roadmap. Practice the click path once before recording.

---

## The click path (what to have ready)

1. Landing page (`/`)
2. Hover **"Try an instant demo →"** → click **Maya · CS @ Georgia Tech** (loads `/dashboard`)
3. **Pin Drop** (`/pin`) — have a screenshot of a flyer/opportunity ready to drag in
4. **Progress Board** (`/progress`)
5. **Success Maps** (`/paths`)
6. **Opportunities Database** (`/opportunities`)

---

## Narration + on-screen actions

### [0:00–0:20] Hook + problem
> **[On screen: landing page]**
>
> "Every student knows internships, research, and fellowships matter. The hard part isn't knowing they matter — it's connecting the dots. The programs that actually get you there are scattered across university pages, LinkedIn posts, and flyers, and most students never see the full path. Miss the moment someone mentions one, and it's just gone.
>
> Sylo is the map."

### [0:20–0:45] Personalized roadmap
> **[On screen: hover "Try an instant demo," click Maya · CS @ Georgia Tech → dashboard loads]**
>
> "A student tells Sylo their goal, major, year, and school — and optionally drops in a resume. Sylo generates a roadmap built for their exact situation. Every step is a real opportunity at their own school, sequenced by what it builds on, what it unlocks, and when the window closes.
>
> **[Point to the top ranked step + gap analysis]**
>
> It doesn't just list options — it ranks the single highest-leverage next move and tells you *why* it matters right now."

### [0:45–1:15] Pin Drop
> **[On screen: go to Pin Drop, drag in a screenshot]**
>
> "Out in the wild, students find opportunities everywhere. Pin Drop keeps the map alive. Drop in any screenshot — a flyer, an Instagram post, an email.
>
> **[Screenshot processes]**
>
> Claude's vision model reads it in seconds, pulls out the deadline and requirements, checks whether you're eligible against your profile, and sorts it by topic automatically. Instead of a camera roll full of screenshots you'll never find again, everything's read, organized, and one tap away from your roadmap."

### [1:15–1:40] Progress Board
> **[On screen: Progress Board — drag a step across columns]**
>
> "Everything flows into one progress board. AI-generated steps and your own pinned finds live side by side — drag them between Not Started, In Progress, and Complete. It's a single place to see exactly where you stand, instead of guessing."

### [1:40–2:00] Success Maps + Database + close
> **[On screen: Success Maps, then Opportunities Database]**
>
> "Success Maps show real roadmaps from students who already made it — and anyone can share the path that got them there. The key programs in those paths feed a curated database of verified opportunities with real deadlines.
>
> **[Return to dashboard or landing]**
>
> When the map is shared, opportunity isn't about who heard first — it's about who's ready to move. That's Sylo. Built on TanStack Start and Claude, no sign-up — from 'I know I should be doing something' to a real plan in under a minute."

---

## Tips
- **Keep energy up and pace brisk** — 2 minutes goes fast; don't linger on any one screen more than ~5 seconds without narration moving.
- **Pre-load the demo persona** right before recording so the roadmap is populated instantly (no waiting on generation on camera).
- **Have the Pin Drop screenshot ready** on your desktop to drag in — the live extraction is the "wow" moment, so let it breathe for a beat.
- **Cut dead air** in post if the vision model takes a second to process; or narrate over the loading state.
- **End on the tagline**, not on a random screen — land the "who's ready to move" line.
