## Describe the problem you identified, your solution, and the impact it would have

**The problem**

Every student knows internships matter. They know research experience matters, that fellowships open doors. Awareness isn't the problem. **Connecting the dots is.**

The path exists, but for most students it stays invisible. So much of it comes down to hearing about the right thing from the right person, at the right place, at the right time — a professor who mentions a fellowship, an upperclassman who forwards a deadline, a flyer you happen to walk past. Miss that moment and you're simply in the dark: you never knew the opportunity existed, never knew you qualified, never knew there was a next step to take. Students can't see the sequence, either — that a research program this fall is what makes them competitive for a fellowship next spring, which unlocks the internship that leads to the full-time offer.

And the information isn't hidden — it's scattered. A fellowship deadline lives on a university page. An internship shows up as a LinkedIn post. A research opportunity is a flyer on a wall or a forwarded email. Students screenshot these things constantly, then lose them in a camera roll of 4,000 photos. By the time they remember, the window closed. Talented students miss opportunities not because they weren't qualified, but because no one drew them a map.

**Our solution**

Sylo draws that map — and it's personalized to *you*. A student shares their goal, major, year, and school, and optionally a resume. Sylo reads all of it and generates a roadmap tailored to their exact situation: a Biology sophomore at UCLA sees different programs than a CS junior at Georgia Tech. Every step is a real opportunity *at their own school*, sequenced by what it builds on, what it opens, and when its window closes — and each one explains why it matters given what that student has already done. A gap analysis, drawn from their resume, names what they have, what's missing, and the single highest-leverage next move.

**Pin Drop** keeps the map alive. A student screenshots anything they find in the wild and drops it in. Claude's vision model reads it in seconds, extracts the deadline and requirements, checks eligibility against their profile, and files it — one tap adds it to their roadmap. It's also your catch-all: instead of scattered screenshots, random saved links, and deadlines lost across notes and browser tabs, everything you drop in gets read, sorted, and organized into one roadmap automatically.

Everything flows into a **progress board**: AI steps and saved pins live side-by-side, dragged between Not Started, In Progress, and Complete. **Success Maps** show real roadmaps from students who already made it. Anyone can share the path that led them to success, and the key opportunities in those paths flow straight into a **Curated Opportunities Database**. So you're not just seeing what worked for someone else — you can find and apply to the same verified programs that got them there.

**The impact**

Sylo brings students out of the dark. It gives every one of them the connective tissue that being in the right place at the right time usually provides: not just *what* opportunities exist, but *how they fit together* and *what to do first*. It turns knowledge that used to depend on luck and access into something anyone can open in a browser.

A freshman who knows "internships matter" but has no idea how to become competitive now sees a sequenced plan built around programs at their own campus, matched to their major and year. A student who would have lost a fellowship deadline in their photos gets it structured, eligibility-checked against their profile, and calendar-ready in five seconds. When the map is shared, opportunity isn't a matter of who heard first — it's a matter of who's ready to move. That's the map we're building for everyone.

Built on TanStack Start and Claude with no sign-up, Sylo takes a student from "I know I should be doing something" to a personalized, actionable plan in under a minute.
